Core formulas

Lead-Time Demand = Daily Demand × Lead Time

Safety Stock = Service-Level Factor × Demand Variability × √Lead Time

Reorder Point = Lead-Time Demand + Safety Stock

Target Stock = Demand × (Lead Time + Review Period) + Safety Stock

Recommended Order = max(0, Target Stock − Current Stock)
